# Era Pop: customer art prompts

Seven prompts, one per sheet. Save each result over `assets/sheets/era0.jpg` ... `era6.jpg`. No code changes are needed.

## How a player reads a customer

Every customer is a **Look** (the era they come from) wearing a **Job** (what they are). For example, "Egyptian Knight" is a knight from ancient Egypt. To keep both obvious at card size:

- **The Job is the costume and the prop**: the silhouette. The headgear and the prop must be the same in every era, so a knight is always a knight.
- **The Look is the colors, materials, face and hair**. A Caveman Knight wears the knight's helmet and sword, but made of stone, fur and bone.

### Job anchors (never change these)

| Job | Headgear | Prop held near the face | Clothes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caveman | wild shaggy hair, no hat | big stone club over the shoulder | fur pelt over one shoulder |
| Farmer | wide woven straw hat | sickle and a bundle of wheat | simple work tunic |
| Knight | helmet with raised visor and a plume | upright sword | chest armor |
| Gentleman | tall top hat | cane and a teacup | jacket, cravat, monocle |
| Greaser | tall slick pompadour, no hat | pocket comb | leather jacket with popped collar, T-shirt |
| Pilot | leather flying cap, goggles pushed up | wooden propeller badge or toy plane | flight jacket with fur collar, long scarf |
| Astronaut | round bubble helmet | small flag or ray-gun shaped scanner | puffy space suit with a chest control panel |

### Look treatments (apply to everything the character wears)

| Look | Palette | Materials and details | Face and hair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caveman | stone grey, earthy brown | stone, fur, bone, leather lashed with rope | unibrow, stubble, messy hair |
| Egyptian | gold, lapis blue | gold, linen, blue-and-gold stripes, scarab and ankh jewelry | kohl-lined eyes, bronze skin |
| Medieval | steel grey, royal purple | chainmail, riveted plates, heraldic tabard with a crest | pageboy haircut |
| Victorian | sepia brown, brass | tweed, check pattern, brass clockwork gears | waxed handlebar mustache, sideburns |
| 1950s | hot pink, teal | polka dots, chrome trim, glossy diner style | slicked hair, big grin |
| Old-School (1920s aviation) | brown leather, olive green | stitched leather, canvas, brass rivets | windswept hair, ruddy cheeks |
| Future | white, navy, glowing cyan | glossy panels, neon circuit lines, holographic visor | sleek hair, cyan eye glints |

## Style block (start every prompt with this)

```
A character sheet of 7 cartoon customer portraits for a casual mobile game. Bold, chunky cartoon style with thick dark outlines, flat bright colors and simple cel shading, like a polished mobile game. Each character is shown from the chest up, facing the viewer, with a big expressive face and a different emotion (happy, smug, grumpy, excited, nervous, proud, surprised). Each character holds their prop up near their face so it is visible at a small size. The headgear and prop must be large and easy to read. Pure white background, no ground, no shadows on the background, no text, no labels, no frames. Arrange exactly 7 characters in two rows: 4 in the top row and 3 in the bottom row, evenly spaced with clear white gaps so no two characters touch or overlap. Every character has a thick dark outline all around it. Characters, in reading order:
```

Then add that sheet's list below.

## Tips

- Generate at a wide 3:2 size (about 1500 x 1000 or bigger). Save as JPG.
- Keep the background pure white. The game removes near-white pixels that touch the background, so every character needs its dark outline.
- Check the order before saving: the era's original first, then the six hybrids in job order (Caveman, Farmer, Knight, Gentleman, Greaser, Pilot, Astronaut, skipping the sheet's own job).
- If a figure comes out touching its neighbor, regenerate. The slicer needs a white gap between all 7.

# Items: one sheet (`assets/sheets/items.jpg`)

All 8 come from a single image, saved over `assets/sheets/items.jpg`. The game slices it into 2 rows of 4, in reading order: the empty bubble, then the 7 items by tier. No code change is needed. Any size works; 3:2 (for example 1536×1024) is ideal.

> A sprite sheet of 8 game item icons for a chunky mobile merge game, in a bold cartoon style: thick dark outlines, rich cel shading with soft highlights, saturated colors, toy-like proportions, three-quarter view. Pure white background, no ground shadows, no text, no labels, no numbers.
>
> Layout: exactly 2 rows of 4 icons, evenly spaced on a wide 3:2 canvas, every icon about the same size, with clear white space between them. No icon touches another or the edge.
>
> Each icon is compact and rounded so it fits inside a circle. Each one should look bigger and fancier than the one before it.
>
> Top row, left to right:
> 1. An empty glossy soap bubble: a perfect circle, pale and almost colorless with a light blue tint, a big curved white shine at the top left, nothing inside.
> 2. A chunky prehistoric stone wheel with a round hole in the middle, chipped edges, cracks and a little moss.
> 3. A small, stubby wooden hand cart with plank sides, one stone wheel, rope tied around it and straw inside.
> 4. A royal carriage: a round purple body with gold trim, a small gold crown on top, curtained windows and four spoked wheels, compact and tall.
>
> Bottom row, left to right:
> 5. A Victorian penny-farthing bicycle: a huge front wheel, a tiny back wheel, a red frame, a brass bell and a leather seat.
> 6. A cute, bubbly 1950s car in teal with lots of chrome, white-wall tires, round headlights and tail fins.
> 7. A 1920s biplane with a red body and yellow wings and a wooden propeller, seen from the front at an angle so it isn't too wide.
> 8. A classic retro rocket in white and red, with fins, a round porthole window and a bright orange flame, tilted slightly.

## Tips for items

- If any two items touch, regenerate: the slicer needs white gaps between them.
- The bubble is tinted a different color per tier in the game, so keep it pale and neutral.
